Using Vocablito

Vocablito is a vocabulary app. You can use it without an account. These terms apply on top of the standard Apple and Google end user licence agreements.

Content you share with others

Tutors can send word lists to students who connected to them, and students share a progress summary and their own example sentences with their tutor. Word lists can also be shared as links. You are responsible for what you send. There is zero tolerance for objectionable or abusive content: no harassment, hate, threats, sexual content involving minors, spam or anything illegal.

Reporting and blocking

Every list from a tutor and every connection has a "Report" button in the app, or write to support@vocablito.com. We review reports within 24 hours, remove offending content and block accounts that break these terms. You can also disconnect from a tutor or a student at any time: they lose access at once and can't reconnect without a new code that you accept.

Your data

See the Privacy Policy. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

No warranty

Vocablito is provided as is. Translations and hints come from dictionaries and may contain mistakes.
